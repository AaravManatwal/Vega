Privacy Policy — Vega (वेग)

Developer: Manatwal Innovations • Effective Date: 2026-11-15
100% Offline Peer-to-Peer • Zero Data Collection

1. No Data Collection or Cloud Storage

Vega transfers files directly between your devices over local Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi Direct, or a temporary local hotspot. Your files, contacts, device identifiers, and transfer history never leave your local network and are never uploaded to external servers.

2. Permissions Used & Why

  • Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (Android 13+, neverForLocation): Used exclusively to discover nearby Vega peers without requesting your GPS or location.
  • Camera: Used strictly on-device to scan QR pairing codes shown by a receiving device. No photos or video frames are recorded or transmitted.
  • Notifications & Foreground Data Sync: Displays transfer progress and keeps large file transfers active when the screen turns off.

3. Local Encryption & Verification

Optional hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption and SHA-256 integrity checks run entirely on your device.
